# Lab Access & Credentials

**AI in Software Testing — August 12–13, 2026**

Two resources to connect to each day: your **ProTech remote lab machine** (where you run all lab exercises) and your **AWS cloud account** (used in Labs 1.3, 2.2, and 2.3).

---

## Step 1 — Connect to Your Lab Machine

1. Open a current browser (Edge, Chrome, Firefox, or Safari).
2. Go to **[https://labs.protechtraining.com](https://labs.protechtraining.com)**.
3. Log in with your **Lab User ID** and password from the table below.
4. Connect to your assigned **Host Computer**.

> The instructor will assign you a student number at the start of Day 1. Find your row in the table.

---

## Step 2 — Log in to AWS

1. Click your **AWS Console Login URL** from the table below (or paste it into a browser on your lab machine).
2. Enter your **AWS Username** and **AWS Console Password**.
3. Confirm you are in the **us-west-1 (N. California)** region — check the region selector in the top-right corner of the AWS Console.

> Your AWS API credentials (Access Key ID and Secret Access Key) are on a separate handout. You will configure them from the lab machine terminal during the relevant labs.

**AWS login fails?**  
Make sure you are using the exact URL from your row — each account has its own sign-in URL. The username is case-sensitive (`Student01`, not `student01`).

**Wrong AWS region?**  
All labs use **us-west-1 (N. California)**. Change it in the region drop-down in the top-right corner of the AWS Console before starting any lab.
