# Lab 10: Capstone End-to-End Modernization

## Module Alignment
Module 10: Capstone End-to-End Modernization

## Timebox
120 minutes

## Objectives
- Deliver a complete modernization slice from planning to operations.
- Demonstrate cross-functional delivery with Dev, QA, and DevOps roles.
- Present architectural and operational trade-offs.

## Prerequisites
- Labs 1 through 9 completed or partially scaffolded.
- Team roles assigned.

## Step-by-Step
1. Create capstone scope and acceptance criteria.
2. Plan work in ADO board with assigned sprint tasks.
3. Refactor one legacy module with Copilot assistance.
4. Add or enhance one SQL-backed API capability.
5. Ensure automated test coverage and quality gate compliance.
6. Build and publish container images.
7. Deploy to OpenShift or Azure-hosted target.
8. Validate telemetry and operational readiness.
9. Conduct final demo and technical retrospective.

## Validation Checks
- All acceptance criteria are met.
- CI/CD pipeline and tests succeed.
- Production-like monitoring is active.

## Deliverables
- Team demo recording or live walkthrough.
- Capstone architecture diagram.
- Lessons learned and phased roadmap.

## Stretch Goals
- Add resilience testing and rollback rehearsal.
